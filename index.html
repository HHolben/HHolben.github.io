About Me

Hello, World! Welcome to my website. My name is Henry Holben, I like to make things and write stuff. I work as a quality engineer for SL Tennessee, an automotive components supplier.

I was born and raised in Johnson City, Tennessee, baptized at First Christian Church in Johnson City, and instilled with common sense at Providence Academy (they gave it their best shot).

In 2024, I graduated from Milligan University with a bachelor's of science degree in electrical engineering with minors in humanities, mathematics, and journalism.

My life goals include designing a board game and participating in a Welsh bog-snorkeling contest. I write science fiction; I occasionally play a cello, and I really, really like airplanes.
