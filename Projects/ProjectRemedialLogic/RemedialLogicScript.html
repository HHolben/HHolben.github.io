Remedial Logic

A dark comedy by Henry Holben



Dramatis Personae

  • Professor: a professor of logic and philosophy at a large university, he secretly despises teaching remedial logic, but hides it well
  • CAMILLA: A remedial logic student with a pocketknife
  • CLAUDIA: An amateur chemist who is in remedial logic class
  • CHLOE: A lethargic student in remedial logic class
  • CAL: A very dedicated e-sports player, who also happens to be taking remedial logic
  • CARLISLE: A shy, timid student in remedial logic class who is probably harboring a terrible secret
  • KYLE: a remedial logic student who might be dead

    • 1. INT. A HALLWAY IN A UNIVERSITY CLASS BUILDING, DAY

    A Professor closes a door behind him, walks down the hallway, and enters a classroom. Professor walks to a whiteboard. CAMILLA is sharpening a bundle of sticks with a pocket knife. KAILEE is sitting with her head on a desk and eyes closed. CLAUDIA is carefully pouring water from one container into another and back again. CAL is playing a video game on a laptop. CARLISLE is coloring in a coloring book. KYLE is frozen in place, holding a pen in his fist to a piece of crumpled paper.

    Professor

    Good morning everyone. Let’s have at it. If you did your reading over the weekend you should all be very familiar the syllogism. As is tradition we’ll start with a classic example- (begins writing syllogism on board) All men are mortal. Socrates is a man. Therefore, Socrates is-

    Professor waves his hand in front of KYLE’S eyes. Professor leans over to read what is on the crumpled sheet of paper. Professor takes the paper. KYLE falls out of his chair. CAL, and CAMILLA look up to see what happened to KYLE, and then resume their respective activities. Professor reads the note carefully.

    Professor (mildly irritated)

    Ok guys, who killed Kyle?

    CAMILLA

    Kyle hasn’t been to class in three weeks!

    CHLOE

    Had it comin’.

    Professor

    Seriously guys. This a violation of the university’s academic integrity policy. That’s a big problem. Who killed Kyle?

    CLAUDIA

    Is he really dead? Kyle always looks dead at 8 AM.

    CAL

    Yeah duh he’s dead- he has blood oozing out his nose. Everyone knows that means your spine’s broken.

    CHLOE

    That’s your ears doofus.

    CAL

    Then how come his spine’s broken? His spine’s broken so there must be blood comin’ out his nose.



    Professor

    Actually Cal, that’s the circular reasoning fallacy. We covered that last chapter.



    CHLOE

    I told you that’s not true.



    Professor

    And that is the argument from fallacy fallacy



    CHLOE

    Aw shoot. Not that.



    CAMILLA

    Kyle bleeds out his nose like all the time.



    KAILEE (points at STUDENT 1)

    Hasty generalization.



    CLAUDIA

    No, actually I’ve seen it too. Bloody nose, all the time



    Professor (quietly)

    Carlisle, go trigger the campus safety alarm outside.



    CARLISLE gets up and bolts for the door.



    Professor

    Wait!



    CARLISLE stops Professor hands CARLISLE a cell phone.



    Professor

    Call the police too.



    CARLISLE almost bolts for the door.





    Professor

    Wait!



    CARLISLE stops



    Professor

    You’d better go find the college president. Give him this.



    Professor hands CARLISLE the crumpled note



    CARLISLE nods and turns for the door.



    Professor

    Hold on!



    CARLISLE stops



    Professor

    Call the cleaning staff, tell them there’s a lot of blood on the floor. That’s all.



    CARLISLE nods and turns for the door.



    Professor

    Oh, wait I almost forgot!



    CARLISLE stops



    Professor (talking quietly)



    Here’s some cash. While you’re at it, could you run by the grill and pick up a coffee for me?



    Professor gives CARLISLE some money.

    Professor

    (Handing over his car keys) And put it in my car.



    CARLISLE nods and runs out the door.



    Professor

    Did anybody notice when Kyle came in?


    CLASS shrugs and shakes heads.



    CAMILLA

    I like, did not even know that Kyle was even in here.



    CAL

    Yeah, I didn’t see him either.



    Professor (suspiciously)

    Alright. Who was in here first?



    CHLOE

    I was.



    Professor

    Did you see or hear anything that might be good for us to know?



    KAILEE

    Don’t ask me. I was pretty late getting in last night. This morning was just a pain to get up, I’m so tired.



    CLAUDIA

    Objection!



    Professor

    What?



    CLAUDIA

    Objection! That’s the appeal to pity fallacy!



    Professor

    Uh- yes, actually that is. Good point.



    CAMILLA

    Kyle used to use the appeal to pity fallacy, like all the time.



    CAL

    You do that more than Kyle



    CAMILLA

    Tu quoque! Tu quoque!



    CAL

    Ah Touché



    Professor (irritated)

    Ok, who came in next?



    CLASS looks around and shrugs



    Professor (furious)

    Nobody knows who murdered Kyle?!



    CAL (starting to take interest)

    Do we even know that Kyle was murdered? Maybe he was just sick.



    CHLOE

    His blood’s all over the floor, doofus!



    CAMILLA

    Kyle was sick like all the time.



    CLAUDIA (annoyed)

    Look, all I know is when I got here, Cammilla, Cal, and Carlisle were in the room. I’m not sure about Kyle



    Professor

    That’s interesting, that contradicts what Chloe told us.



    CHLOE

    Huh?



    Professor

    (pointing at Chloe) You said you were here first. But CLAUDIA just said you weren’t here when she came in. I was going to wait until next week to start truth tables, but a student is dead. (goes to whiteboard). Let’s call CLAUDIA’s statement statement A, and KAILEE’s statement B (begins drawing table). If A is true then B is false, and if B is true A, then what do we know?



    CAMILLA

    If B is like false then A is like true



    Professor

    Good try Camilla, that would be the converse of the statement which is-



    CAL

    Claudia killed Kyle?!



    CLAUDIA looks up and starts to take notice of her surroundings



    Professor

    Uh, no we can’t prove that.



    CAL

    Chloe killed Kyle?



    CHLOE

    Nope.



    PROFESSOR

    Well see we don’t know-



    CAL

    Camilla killed Kyle?



    CAMILLA

    I like, did not even like talking to Kyle



    There is a long pause while the class thinks



    PROFESSOR (pointing to board)

    What can we conclude about-?



    CAL

    Kyle killed Camilla?



    CLAUDIA

    Hey we’re did CARLISLE go?



    There is a long pause while Professor contemplates this. The STUDENTS look around to try to figure out where CARLISLE went.



    Professor

    Carlisle.



    Professor looks at wristwatch and bolts out the door.
